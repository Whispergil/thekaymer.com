Privacy Policy

Placely — a mobile application operated by Kaymer LLC

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Placely ("Placely," "we," "us," or "our") is a mobile application operated by Kaymer LLC ("Kaymer") that helps you privately save and remember places you care about. This Privacy Policy explains what information Placely collects, how it is used and stored, who it may be shared with, and the choices available to you, including how to delete your account.

This policy applies only to the Placely mobile application. It does not apply to the thekaymer.com website, which has its own Privacy Policy, or to any other app published by Kaymer LLC.

1. Information We Collect

Account Data

When you create a Placely account, we may collect and store:

  • Email address
  • Display name
  • Profile photo URL, when supplied by your authentication provider
  • Authentication provider information (which sign-in method(s) are linked to your account)
  • Account creation and last-updated timestamps

Placely supports the following authentication methods: Email & Password, Sign in with Apple, and Google Sign-In. If you use Email & Password, your password is handled directly by Firebase Authentication and is never stored by Placely in our own database (Cloud Firestore).

Saved Place Data

Placely's core feature is letting you save places. When you add a place, we may store:

  • Place name
  • Category
  • Notes
  • Country, state/province, city, address, and postal code
  • Latitude, longitude, and GPS accuracy
  • Favorite status
  • A photo of the place, if you add one
  • Record creation and last-updated timestamps

Location Information

Placely requests your device's location only in the foreground and only when you take an action that needs it, such as adding a place or taking a place photo. Placely does not track your location in the background, and it does not collect or store your location unless you choose to save it with a place. Coordinates you save may be sent to Google Maps to render map views within the app.

Photos

Camera and photo library access is user-initiated — Placely only accesses your camera or photo library when you choose to take or select a photo for a saved place. Photos you capture or select are uploaded to Firebase Storage and associated with that saved place. Placely does not use any audio or video recording feature.

Information Stored on Your Device

Some information stays on your device only and is not transmitted to us. This may include:

  • Recent search terms
  • Theme preference (light/dark/system)
  • Other app preferences
  • Firebase authentication session data, so you stay signed in between app launches

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to operate Placely's core functionality: creating and securing your account, letting you save, organize, and revisit places, displaying maps and photos, and providing customer support when you contact us.

3. Third-Party Services

Placely is built on the following third-party services, which process data as needed to provide their respective functions:

  • Google Firebase — Placely's backend platform, including Firebase Authentication (account sign-in and session management), Cloud Firestore (storing account and place data), and Firebase Storage (storing place photos). See Firebase's privacy and security information.
  • Google Sign-In — used if you choose to sign in with your Google account. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Sign in with Apple — used if you choose to sign in with your Apple ID. See Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Maps Platform — used to display maps and the locations of your saved places within the app. See Google's Privacy Policy.

We only share the information with these providers that is necessary for them to perform the function described above (for example, Google or Apple receive the standard sign-in information their authentication flow requires when you choose to use it). Placely does not sell your personal information or share your saved-place data with third parties for advertising purposes. Third-party services process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

4. What Placely Does Not Do

As of the date of this policy, Placely:

  • Does not contain advertising
  • Does not sell your personal data
  • Does not contain a third-party analytics or tracking SDK
  • Does not track your location in the background
  • Does not currently provide functional push notifications
  • Does not use Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Performance Monitoring, or Firebase Cloud Functions

If any of this changes in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy accordingly.

5. Data Storage & Security

We use access controls designed to restrict account and saved-place data to the authenticated account owner. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and while we work to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete an individual saved place at any time from within the app. You can also permanently delete your entire Placely account from Settings. Account deletion is intended to permanently remove your Placely-hosted saved-place photos, place records, and user profile data, along with your Firebase Authentication account itself.

Because sign-in with Google or Apple is handled by those providers directly, any data they retain about your use of their sign-in service remains subject to their own privacy policies and is not deleted by Placely's account-deletion process.

7. Children's Privacy

Placely is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the email below so we can address it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and provide any additional notice required by applicable law.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us below. You can permanently delete your Placely account directly in the app under Profile → Settings → Delete Account.
