Placely Support

Placely — a mobile application operated by Kaymer LLC

Need help with Placely? We're here to help.

The fastest way to reach us is by email. We read every message and typically respond within a few business days.

Email support@thekaymer.com

Account & Sign-In

You can sign in to Placely with Email & Password, Sign in with Apple, or Google Sign-In. If you're having trouble signing in, forgot your password, or want to link or unlink a sign-in method, check the sign-in options available in the app first. If you're still stuck, email us at support@thekaymer.com and let us know which sign-in method you're using.

Saving Places

Placely lets you save a place with a name, category, notes, address details, and location. You can mark places as favorites and revisit them anytime from the app. If a saved place isn't showing up correctly or you're having trouble adding or editing one, try closing and reopening the app first — if the issue continues, reach out to support.

Location & Photos

Placely may ask for permission to access your device's location and camera or photo library when you add a place — these are only used when you take that action, and location is never tracked in the background. If Placely isn't able to detect your location or access your camera/photos, check that the relevant permission is enabled for Placely in your device's system settings.

Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your Placely account and its data at any time, directly from within the app:

  • Open Placely and go to Profile
  • Tap Settings
  • Tap Delete Account and follow the confirmation steps

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. See our Privacy Policy for details on what is removed.
