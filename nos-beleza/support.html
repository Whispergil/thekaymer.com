Nôs Beleza is a local services marketplace for Cabo Verde, starting with beauty and personal care. Customers find nearby businesses, browse their services, and request an appointment. Professionals publish a storefront, set their services and schedule, and manage bookings from an agenda.

This page covers the questions that come up most. If it does not answer yours, email support@thekaymer.com or use Support inside the app, which lets us see your account and reply to you in the app.

Booking an appointment

Choose your island, browse or search businesses, open one, pick a service, and choose from the times the business has open. You can add a note if there is something the professional should know.

A booking is a request, not an instant confirmation. The business can confirm it, decline it, or propose a different time. You will get a notification when they respond, and you can follow the appointment under Appointments.

If a business proposes a new time, open the booking and accept the new time or keep your original request.

Changing or cancelling a booking

Open the booking under Appointments. From there you can cancel it, or ask the business to move it to another time. Cancel as early as you can so the slot goes back to someone else.

A business can also cancel or ask to reschedule. You will get a notification if that happens.

Payment

Nôs Beleza does not take payment. You pay the professional directly, by whatever method you agree with them. Prices shown on a listing are set by the business as an indication of what it charges. If something goes wrong with a payment, that is between you and the business, though you can report a problem to us.

Signing in and your account

You must be at least 18 to use Nôs Beleza. You can sign in with an email address and password, with Apple, or with Google.

Forgot your password? Tap "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen and we will email you a reset link.

Signed up with Apple or Google and cannot get in? Use the same provider you originally signed up with. If you signed up with Apple and later try to sign in with Google using the same email address, they are separate sign-in methods. Sign in the original way, then add the other method under Account and security.

Used Hide My Email with Apple? Apple gives us a private relay address instead of your real one. It works normally and is the address we will use to reach you.

Changing your email or password. Both are under Account and security. You will be asked to confirm who you are first.

For professionals: your business

Setting up. Create your business, add your services with their prices and durations, and set your working schedule. Your storefront only appears to customers once you publish it.

Managing bookings. New requests arrive in your Agenda. Confirm, decline, or propose a new time. Mark an appointment complete after the service so it moves into your history and the customer can leave a review.

Your schedule. Availability comes from the working hours you set and the duration of each service, so keeping both accurate is what stops double bookings.

Pausing bookings. Unpublish your storefront to stop new requests coming in while you deal with the ones you already have.

Photos and your storefront

You can add a logo, a cover image, and portfolio photographs under Business Appearance. The app will ask for permission to reach your photo library or camera the first time.

Photos not uploading? Check your connection, then check that Nôs Beleza has photo access in your device settings. Very large images can take a moment on a slow connection.

Replacing an image. Uploading a new logo or cover replaces the old one, and the previous file is removed.

Only upload photographs you have the right to use, and do not include a customer's face without their permission.

Verification

You can apply to have your business verified by submitting supporting documents. Those documents are private: they are never shown on your public listing, and only you and the Kaymer staff reviewing the application can see them. We keep them no longer than 90 days after a final decision, then delete them.

A verified badge means we reviewed documents at that time. It is not a guarantee or an endorsement of the business's work.

Notifications

Nôs Beleza sends notifications about your own bookings — a new request, a confirmation, a decline, a proposed time, a cancellation, and reminders before an appointment.

Not receiving them? Work through these in order:

  • Check that notifications are allowed for Nôs Beleza in your device settings.
  • Check your notification preferences inside the app under Settings.
  • Make sure you are signed in. Notifications are tied to your account, so signing out stops them on that device.
  • Open the app once while connected. Registration happens after you sign in.

If you share a device, note that signing out removes that device's registration, so the next person does not get your appointments.

Reviews

After an appointment is marked complete you can leave a rating and a review of that business. A review shows your first name at most, never your full name or email address.

Reviews cannot be edited or deleted after they are published, and a business can publish one response to each review. Please write carefully.

Safety and reporting a problem

You can report a business, a review, or another user from inside the app. Choose the category that fits and tell us what happened. Reports go only to us.

If you are in immediate danger, contact the local emergency services first. We are an app support team and cannot respond to an emergency.

Nôs Beleza is a marketplace. The professionals are independent businesses, not our staff, so use the same judgement you would when choosing any service provider.

Deleting your account

Open Account and security in the app and choose to delete your account. You will be asked to confirm who you are. You do not need to email us.

What is removed: your sign-in credentials, your account and profile, your saved favourites, and your notification registrations on every device.

What stays: reviews you wrote stay published but are disconnected from you; bookings, support conversations, and reports are kept for a limited period as business records. If you run a business, deleting your account closes your storefront, but the listing and its images are not removed automatically — email us if you want those deleted too.

If you run a business with appointments still outstanding, resolve them first. The app will not finish the deletion while customers are waiting on you.

The full detail is in the Privacy Policy.

Contact us

Email support@thekaymer.com, or use Support inside the app so we can see your account and reply in the app.

Please do not send passwords or payment details.
