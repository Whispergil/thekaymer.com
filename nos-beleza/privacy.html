Nôs Beleza ("Nôs Beleza," "we," "us," or "our") is a mobile application operated by Kaymer LLC ("Kaymer"). Nôs Beleza is a local services marketplace for Cabo Verde, starting with beauty and personal care. It connects customers with independent beauty professionals and businesses. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used and stored, who it is shared with, and the choices available to you, including how to delete your account.

This policy applies only to the Nôs Beleza mobile application. It does not apply to the thekaymer.com website, which has its own Privacy Policy, or to any other app published by Kaymer LLC.

Nôs Beleza does not provide beauty services. The professionals listed in the app are independent businesses. Information you share with a professional when you book an appointment is described in section 4.

1. Information You Give Us

Account information. When you create an account we collect your name, your email address, and the island in Cabo Verde you choose. If you sign up with an email address and password, your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication and is never stored by us in a form we can read.

Sign in with Apple and Google. If you choose Apple or Google to sign in, we receive a unique identifier for your account with that provider, and the name and email address that provider releases to us. If you use Apple's Hide My Email, we receive the private relay address Apple generates rather than your real address, and that relay address is the only one we will have. We do not receive your password from Apple or Google.

Business information (professionals only). If you set up a business on Nôs Beleza, we collect the business name, description, category, island and location details, contact details you choose to publish, your services with their prices and durations, and your working schedule.

Photos. Professionals can upload a business logo, a cover image, and portfolio photographs. These are chosen by you from your device's photo library or camera.

Verification documents (professionals only). If you apply to have your business verified, we collect the documents and evidence you submit for that purpose.

Bookings. When you request an appointment we record the business, the service, the date and time, and any note you choose to add.

Reviews. After a completed appointment you may leave a rating and written review.

Support requests and reports. If you contact support or report a business, a review, or another user, we collect the message you send and the category you choose.

2. Information Collected Automatically

We keep this deliberately small. The app does not include analytics or advertising software, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Notification tokens. If you allow notifications, we store a push notification token issued by Apple and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, together with the platform (iOS or Android), a randomly generated identifier for that installation of the app, and the app version. The installation identifier is generated on your device and is not derived from your device's hardware identifiers or from anything about you.

Technical information handled by our service providers. Google Firebase processes technical information such as IP addresses in the ordinary course of operating the service, for example to deliver data to your device and to protect against abuse. This is described in section 5.

Timestamps. Records such as accounts, bookings, reviews, and support messages carry the date and time they were created or changed.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information above to operate Nôs Beleza: to create and maintain your account, to show you businesses and services on your island, to place and manage bookings, to let professionals run their storefront and schedule, to publish reviews, to send you notifications about your own bookings, to review verification applications, to respond to support requests, to investigate reports and enforce our Terms of Service, and to keep the service secure and working.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not build advertising profiles.

4. What Other People Can See

This section matters more in a marketplace than in most apps, so it is set out plainly.

Public to anyone using the app. A published business listing, including its name, description, category, island, the contact details the professional chose to publish, its services and prices, its logo, cover image, and portfolio photographs, and its verification badge if it has one. Reviews are also public: a review shows the rating, the written text, and a reviewer label that is at most the reviewer's first name. A public review does not carry the reviewer's account identifier, full name, or email address.

Shared with the professional you book. When you request an appointment, the professional running that business sees your display name, your email address, the service, the date and time, and any note you added. This is how they know who is coming and can reach you about the appointment. Please do not put anything in a booking note that you would not want that business to hold.

Shared with the customer. The professional's business name and published details.

Private to you. Your saved favourites, your booking history, your own account settings, and the record linking you to the reviews you wrote.

Private to us and the submitting business. Verification documents. These are never shown on a public listing. Only the business that submitted them and Kaymer staff reviewing the application can access them.

Private to us. Support messages, reports, and notification tokens.

5. Service Providers and Where Data Is Processed

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing. We do rely on service providers to run the app, and they process data on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC) provides authentication, the database, file storage, server functions, and push notification delivery. Substantially all of the data described in this policy is stored and processed using Firebase.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and delivers push notifications to iOS devices through the Apple Push Notification service.
  • Google provides Google Sign-In.

International processing. Nôs Beleza serves Cabo Verde, but the infrastructure it runs on is not located there. Our server functions run in Google's us-central1 region in the United States, and Firebase data and uploaded files are stored on Google infrastructure outside Cabo Verde. If you use Nôs Beleza, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States and potentially other countries where Google operates. Those countries may not provide the same level of data protection as Cabo Verde.

We may also disclose information where we are legally required to, or where we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect the rights or safety of a person or to investigate fraud or abuse.

6. How Long We Keep Information

Nôs Beleza runs an automated retention process that deletes older records on a schedule:

  • Completed, cancelled, and declined bookings are deleted about two years after they were finished. A booking that is still active or unresolved is never deleted on age alone.
  • Resolved support conversations are deleted about one year after they were resolved. An open conversation is not deleted.
  • Reports are deleted about two years after they were made.
  • Verification documents are deleted about 90 days after a final decision is made on the application. The decision itself, and whether the business is verified, is kept.
  • Notification delivery records are deleted after about 90 days.

Reviews do not expire. A published review stays visible so the rating history of a business remains honest over time. Reviews do not carry the reviewer's identity, as described in section 4.

Information we still need to operate your account, such as your account record and a business listing, is kept for as long as the account exists.

7. Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account from inside the app, under Account and security. You do not need to email us and you do not need to ask permission.

What deleting your account removes. Your sign-in credentials, your account record, your customer profile, your saved favourites, and every push notification token registered to your account on every device.

What deleting your account changes but does not remove. The private record linking you to the reviews you wrote is stripped of your account identifier, so your published reviews remain but are no longer connected to you.

What is not removed, and why. Please read this before deleting your account:

  • Bookings you made are kept until the retention period in section 6 expires. A booking record includes the display name and email address you had when you made it. These are the professional's business records of appointments they performed, and they are also how a professional can resolve a dispute about work they did.
  • Reviews you wrote stay published, without your identity attached.
  • Support conversations and reports are kept until the retention periods in section 6 expire.
  • If you run a business, deleting your account closes your storefront so it stops taking new bookings and removes your access to it, but the business record and the images uploaded to it are not automatically deleted. If you want your business listing and its photographs removed, contact us at the address in section 11 and say so.

If you run a business and still have unresolved bookings, the app will not complete the deletion until those are resolved, so that customers who are waiting on an appointment are not left without anyone to deal with.

8. Your Choices and Rights

You can review and change your account details, your island, and your notification preferences in the app at any time. You can turn off notifications for Nôs Beleza in your device settings. You can delete your account as described in section 7.

If you want a copy of the personal information we hold about you, or want information corrected or deleted beyond what the app does on its own, contact us at the address in section 11 and we will respond. Depending on where you live, local law may give you additional rights over your personal information.

9. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored using Google Firebase, which encrypts data at rest. Access to the database and to uploaded files is governed by server-side security rules, so that private records such as verification documents, support messages, and another person's bookings are not readable by other users of the app. Administrative access is limited to a signed server-side credential rather than anything a user account can grant itself.

No service can promise perfect security, and we do not. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal information, we will act to address it and will notify you where we are required to do so.
