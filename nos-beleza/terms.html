A professional account exists to run a business. It is not a customer account with extra screens, and it cannot be used to make customer bookings — including bookings with other businesses.

If you run a business and also want to book an appointment for yourself as a customer, open a separate customer account. The app does not provide a "book as myself" mode inside the professional workspace.

The appointment itself is an agreement between the customer and the business. Kaymer LLC is not a party to it.

9. Appointments

A customer requests an appointment; the business decides. A request is not a confirmed appointment until the business confirms it. A business may confirm a request, decline it, or propose a different time.

Bookings can be cancelled, and a business may propose a reschedule which the customer can accept or decline. The app records the state of a booking as it changes and notifies the people involved.

The availability shown in the app comes from the schedule a business publishes: its working hours, its closures, its buffer time, its daily limit, and how far ahead it accepts bookings. Availability can change, and a business can become unavailable after a booking is made.

Kaymer LLC does not guarantee that a request will be accepted, that a confirmed appointment will take place, that it will start on time, or that it will be carried out as described. Cancellation, lateness, and no-show arrangements are a matter between the customer and the business.

Nôs Beleza does not process payments. The app does not take payment for an appointment, does not store or handle card details, does not hold a deposit, does not pay professionals, and does not charge a commission on a booking. There is no payment provider integrated into the app.

Any payment for a service is arranged and settled directly between the customer and the business, outside Nôs Beleza. Kaymer LLC is not involved in it, holds no record of it, and is not responsible for it. We do not take part in payment disputes, we do not issue refunds for services, and we have no ability to reverse a payment made outside the app.

Agree the price and the payment arrangement with the business before your appointment.

If a business's published price is wrong, that is a matter between you and the business. Please also report it to us so we can follow up on the listing.

12. Reviews and User Content

A customer may review a business after a booking, once per booking. A business may publish a reply to a review of it.

A review must be honest, must describe your own experience, and must not contain abuse, harassment, threats, discriminatory content, unlawful material, someone else's personal information, or anything you know to be false. Do not submit a review in exchange for payment or a discount, do not review your own business, and do not review a competitor's.

Deleting your account does not remove a published review or its attribution; it unlinks the review from your account, as described in the Privacy Policy. Write accordingly.

Do not upload photos of work that is not yours, stock images presented as your own work, or content that is sexual, graphic, unlawful, or infringing.

Those controls are described in the Privacy Policy; they are how the app is built, not a guarantee against every possible failure.
