1. Who We Are

Nôs Beleza is operated by Kaymer LLC. It is a local services marketplace for Cabo Verde, starting with beauty and personal care. How we handle your information is described in our Privacy Policy.

Nôs Beleza is a marketplace. We do not provide beauty services.

The professionals and businesses listed in the app are independent. They are not our employees, agents, partners, or contractors. We do not employ them, supervise their work, set their prices, control their availability, train them, or direct how they perform a service.

When you book an appointment through Nôs Beleza, any agreement for that service is between you and the professional. We are not a party to it. Our role is limited to providing the app that lets you find each other, request a time, and communicate about it.

This means we are not responsible for the quality, safety, legality, timing, or outcome of any service a professional performs, and we do not guarantee that any professional will accept a booking, keep an appointment, or perform to any particular standard.

You need an account to book appointments or run a business. You must give accurate information and keep it up to date, and you are responsible for what happens under your account. Do not share your sign-in credentials. Tell us promptly if you believe someone else has accessed your account.

If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we will close it.

One person should keep one account. You may delete your account at any time from Account and security in the app.

4. Booking Appointments

Bookings on Nôs Beleza work as requests, not instant confirmations:

  • You request an appointment for a service at a date and time the business has available.
  • The business can confirm it, decline it, or propose a different time.
  • If a new time is proposed, you can accept it or keep your original request.
  • Either side can cancel, and either side can ask to reschedule.
  • The business marks the appointment complete after the service.

An appointment is only agreed once the business confirms it. A requested appointment is not a commitment by anyone until then. If you cannot attend a confirmed appointment, cancel it in the app so the time is released.

5. Prices and Payment

Nôs Beleza does not process payments. The app does not take payment for services, does not hold money, and does not handle refunds. Prices shown on a listing are set and published by the business as an indication of what it charges.

Any payment for a service is arranged directly between you and the professional, outside the app, by whatever method you agree. Questions about price, payment, deposits, or refunds are between you and the business. We are not able to refund a payment we never received, and we are not responsible for payment disputes, though you can report a problem to us under section 10.

  • honouring the appointments you confirm, and telling customers promptly when you cannot;
  • having the right to use every image you upload; and
  • your own tax and reporting obligations.

You must only list a business you are authorised to represent. Keep your published prices and availability accurate: a listing that is out of date wastes a customer's time and undermines the marketplace for everyone.

A verified badge means only that a business submitted documents to us and that we reviewed them at that time. It is not a warranty about the quality of the business's work, its licensing status on any later date, its safety practices, or its conduct. We do not audit or inspect businesses, and we do not re-check verification continuously.

You should use your own judgement when choosing a professional, exactly as you would when choosing one outside the app. We may grant, refuse, or withdraw verification at our discretion.

You may review a business after an appointment you actually had with it. Reviews must be honest and based on your own experience. Do not post a review in exchange for payment or a discount, do not review your own business or a competitor's, and do not post a review you know to be false.

A business may publish one response to a review. Reviews and responses are published as written and cannot be edited or removed afterwards, so please write carefully.

Everything you post — reviews, responses, business descriptions, photographs, and messages — must be your own or something you have the right to use, and must not be unlawful, hateful, harassing, deceptive, obscene, or an invasion of someone else's privacy.
