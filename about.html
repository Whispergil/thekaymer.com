About

An independent app studio.

Kaymer LLC builds focused mobile tools for everyday work and life.

Kaymer LLC is an independent mobile app studio based in the United States. We design and build our own apps around practical needs, with a focus on simplicity, usefulness, and thoughtful development.

Findry helps contractors and field crews document jobsites, save photos and measurements, and keep reference points for underground work—making important project information easier to find when it’s needed.

Nôs Beleza is our beauty-services platform in development for Cape Verde, designed to help people discover local businesses and connect with beauty professionals.

We keep our focus on a small collection of apps, improving each one through careful testing, real-world feedback, and ongoing updates.

What that means in practice

Built for real tasks

We focus on features that help people organize information, save time, and make everyday work easier.

Clear about availability

We clearly distinguish available apps and features from those still in development.

Clear about data

Each released app has its own privacy policy explaining how information is collected, used, stored, and managed.
