Kaymer LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website thekaymer.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you visit our website.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information through a form on this website. thekaymer.com has no contact form. If you choose to contact us by email, we receive the information you decide to include in your message, which may be:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your message
  • Any other information you choose to include in the email

This information is sent directly to our email inbox and is used solely to respond to your inquiry.

2. Automatically Collected Information

The Kaymer website is hosted through GitHub Pages, a static-site hosting service provided by GitHub. GitHub processes information according to its GitHub Privacy Statement.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide only to respond to your questions or inquiries and provide support related to our apps. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Cookies

Kaymer does not currently use cookies or analytics on this website. The website is hosted through GitHub Pages, and GitHub processes information according to its GitHub Privacy Statement.

5. Data Retention

Emails you send us are retained in our email inbox only as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry.

6. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Links to Other Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information you have provided to us. Contact us at the email below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of our website after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
