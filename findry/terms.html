2. DESCRIPTION OF THE SERVICE

Findry is a field documentation and jobsite memory application designed to help construction professionals, contractors, tradespeople, maintenance personnel, and other field workers organize important project information.

Depending on the features you use, Findry allows you to:

  • Create and organize projects.
  • Save field records.
  • Attach photos to records.
  • Store measurements and reference points.
  • Record notes, materials, and warning information.
  • Track important installation details.
  • Retrieve previously saved field information.

Findry is intended solely as a documentation tool.

4. FREE PLAN AND FINDRY PRO

Findry offers both a Free Plan and an optional paid Findry Pro subscription.

Free Plan

The Free Plan currently includes:

  • Up to 3 projects
  • Up to 10 total records
  • Up to 3 photos per record
  • Up to 2 reference points per record
  • Local storage on your device

If you reach the Free Plan limit, you can delete existing records to free up space for new ones or upgrade to Findry Pro for unlimited records.

Free Plan limits may change as Findry evolves.

Findry Pro

Findry Pro currently includes:

  • Unlimited projects
  • Unlimited records
  • Up to 100 photos per record
  • Unlimited reference points
  • Secure cloud backup of supported project and record data
  • Access to current and future Findry Pro features included with your subscription

Photos attached to records remain stored locally on your device and are not currently included in cloud backup.
