Kaymer LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website thekaymer.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you visit our website, including the free calculators and guides published on it. Our mobile apps are covered by their own privacy policies.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information through a form on this website. thekaymer.com has no contact form. If you choose to contact us by email, we receive the information you decide to include in your message, which may be:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your message
  • Any other information you choose to include in the email

This information is sent directly to our email inbox and is used solely to respond to your inquiry.

2. Automatically Collected Information

The Kaymer website is hosted through GitHub Pages, a static-site hosting service provided by GitHub. GitHub processes information according to its GitHub Privacy Statement. If you accept analytics cookies, we also use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website, as described in Section 4.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide only to respond to your questions or inquiries and provide support related to our apps. We use analytics information only in summary form, to see which pages are useful, how visitors find our website, and what to improve. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Analytics and Cookies

We use Google Analytics 4, a service provided by Google LLC, to understand how visitors use our website. Google Analytics runs only if you choose "Accept" in the cookie notice shown on your first visit. If you choose "Decline," or make no choice, Google Analytics is not loaded and no analytics cookies are set.

When you accept, Google Analytics collects information such as:

  • the pages you view and how long you stay
  • the website or search that brought you here (referrer)
  • your approximate location (country, region, or city), estimated from your IP address
  • your device type, operating system, browser, and screen size

Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. We do not use Google Analytics to identify you personally, and we do not send it your name, email address, or other personal details.

Google Analytics uses first-party cookies named _ga and _ga_[ID] to tell visits apart. These cookies are set only after you accept and expire after up to 2 years. We store your cookie choice in your browser's local storage so we don't ask again on every page. This stays on your device and is not sent to us.

We set Google Analytics to keep detailed analytics data for 2 months, after which Google deletes it automatically. Summary reports, such as total visits per page, do not identify individual visitors and may be kept longer. We have turned off Google signals, advertising features, and remarketing, and we do not use analytics information for advertising.

You can change your choice at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page. You can also block Google Analytics on all websites by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, or by blocking cookies in your browser settings.

Google processes analytics information according to the Google Privacy Policy. You can read more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages, and GitHub processes information according to its GitHub Privacy Statement.

5. Data Retention

Emails you send us are retained in our email inbox only as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry. Detailed Google Analytics data is deleted automatically after 2 months, as described in Section 4.

6. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Links to Other Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information you have provided to us. Contact us at the email below. You can withdraw your analytics consent at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of our website after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
