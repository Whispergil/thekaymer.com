ABOUT

Built for real work and everyday life.

Kaymer LLC creates focused mobile products that solve practical problems through clear, thoughtful design.

Based in the United States, we build technology around the way people actually work and live. Every product begins with a real need and is shaped to be useful, dependable, and easy to understand.

What we’re building

Findry

Findry helps contractors and field crews organize important jobsite information—including photos, measurements, notes, and project details—and use practical tools such as the Material Calculator and Truck Load Calculator. It is available for iPhone and Android.

Nôs Beleza

Nôs Beleza is an upcoming local services marketplace for Cape Verde, beginning with beauty and personal care. It is currently in development and is not yet available in app stores.
