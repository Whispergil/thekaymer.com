About

An independent app studio.

Kaymer LLC builds focused mobile tools for everyday work and life.

Kaymer LLC is an independent mobile app studio in the United States. Support email goes to the people who build the apps. There is no queue and no call centre.

Findry is available now for iPhone and Android. It keeps jobsite records for contractors and field crews: photos, depths, measurements, buried utility details, and notes, organised by project.

Nôs Beleza is in development. It is a local services marketplace for Cape Verde, starting with beauty and personal care, and it does not have a store listing yet.
